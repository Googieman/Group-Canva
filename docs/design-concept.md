# Canvas surface concept

Mode: Operate. The primary action is making a mark, then seeing another person's mark arrive in the same space. The first viewport must communicate a usable shared board with tool choice, presence, invite, connection, and shared history.

## Approved direction

Warm white canvas, rounded floating tools, bright colors, colorful participant chips, and the compact interaction conventions of Excalidraw and tldraw. This direction was supplied as approved in the implementation brief; product and aesthetic decisions were not reopened. The Impeccable direction seed was run and returned degraded without catalog challengers. Its assignment does not override the pinned direction.

## First viewport

A small brand on the left and real presence plus an invite button on the right. A fitted 16:9 canvas occupies the central area. A restrained empty invitation and geometric mark vanish once drawing begins. The toolbar below contains brush, eraser, seven colors, three widths, and shared undo/redo. The footer explains the shared history behavior.

## Higgsfield preflight

On 2026-09-09, the selected Higgsfield workspace returned **0 credits**, free plan, no active unlimited allowance. `nano_banana_pro` cost preflight returned **2 credits** for one image. The available free generation was for video motion/object tools, not a static concept or illustration. A concept and companion illustration therefore could not be produced within the existing-credit constraint. No generation job, trial, subscription, or billing change was submitted. The result uses honest original CSS and SVG geometry, documented as such.

## Interaction contract

The invite control copies the current room URL, with useful manual-copy fallback. Presence opens an actual participant list. B/E change tools; Ctrl/Cmd Z, Shift Z, and Y operate shared history. Editing inputs keep their native keyboard behavior. Empty artwork cannot intercept drawing. The core ink canvas never depends on an image asset.

## Responsive tradeoff

The same complete board fits at every width. On narrow portrait screens this makes the canvas shorter, preserving all shared coordinates without cropping or hidden drawing. The toolbar wraps into two rows and the invitation action becomes an icon. Local pinch/pan/zoom is outside this MVP.
